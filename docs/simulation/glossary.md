# Glossary

Each term is used in the docs exactly as defined here. Terms written in `code` are `.fea` keywords, spelled as the compiler spells them.

## Studies and runs

**Simulation and study.** "Simulation" names the engineering task in page titles and the RunMat agent's replies. "Study" names the `.fea` construct: a `study` declaration that binds geometry, mesh, model and analysis steps, and the run that executes it.

**Analysis step (procedure).** One entry in a study's analysis sequence, such as `static`, `modal`, `transient`, `harmonic`, `buckling`, `response_spectrum` or `random_response`. Steps can run in order with `after` and pass state to one another. See the [procedure reference](./language/reference/procedures.md).

**Physics family.** The equations a step solves: structural, thermal, electromagnetic, acoustic, fluid, and the coupled families thermomechanical, electrothermal, conjugate heat transfer and fluid–structure interaction. Which procedures each family runs is listed in the [family and procedure matrix](./language/reference/procedures.md#families-and-procedures).

**Case and sweep.** A case is one combination of input values. A sweep is a declared set of cases, combined as a product or element by element (`zip`).

**Run and result.** A run is one execution of a study. Its result is published, content-addressed and reopenable with its provenance.

**`runmat check`, `runmat plan`, `runmat run`.** `check` reports every error in a `.fea` or `.m` source without solving. `plan` shows the resolved execution: mesh, procedures, resources and placement. `run` executes the study and publishes the result.

## Model

**Model.** The `model` declaration: material assignments, constraints, loads, excitations, interfaces, connectors and initial conditions.

**Region.** A named selection of geometry: solids, faces, edges or vertices. Names come from the CAD file itself (`select name` for STEP and IGES labels, `select tag` for layers) or from canonical persistent ids. Inside the solver, every region is matched by its canonical id.

**Canonical persistent id.** The stable identity of a geometric entity, written `persistent-entity:sha256:…`. It survives re-import of the same CAD revision.

**Constraint.** A condition on the unknown field: a fixed or prescribed value (essential, or Dirichlet), a symmetry, a periodic pair or a linear equation between degrees of freedom.

**Load and excitation.** A load is a source term or boundary flux: force, pressure, heat flux, current. An excitation drives a load through time or frequency with a signal.

**Signal.** A typed function of time, frequency or a parameter, written inline, as a standard waveform, as a table or generated by a `.m` function.

**Interface.** A relation between two regions: bonded, contact, a thermal or fluid–structure coupling.

## Discretization

**Mesh.** The finite-element subdivision of the geometry: tetrahedra for solids, triangles for shells, lines for beams.

**Element order.** Linear (first-order) or quadratic (second-order) shape functions. Tet4 is the 4-node linear tetrahedron and Tet10 the 10-node quadratic tetrahedron.

**Degree of freedom (DOF).** One scalar unknown in the assembled system, such as one displacement component at one node.

**Shape function.** The polynomial $N_a$ that interpolates a field from the values at an element's nodes.

**Weak form.** The integral statement of a boundary-value problem: find $u \in \mathcal V$ such that $a(u, v) = \ell(v)$ for all test functions $v \in \mathcal V_0$. Each physics reference page states its family's weak form.

**Stiffness, mass and damping matrices.** $\mathbf K$, $\mathbf M$ and $\mathbf C_d$, assembled from element contributions $\mathbf K^e$, $\mathbf M^e$ and $\mathbf C_d^e$.

## Results

**Field.** A published spatial result at a location: node, element, integration point, face, edge, interface, connector, constraint, reference point, mode or global. It is written `analysis.<step>.field.<location>.<quantity>`.

**Output (observation).** An authored reduction of a field to the quantity the study is asked to answer, such as `mean`, `max`, `interpolate`, `integral` or `resultant`.

**Von Mises stress.** The equivalent stress $\sigma_{vM} = \sqrt{\tfrac{3}{2}\, \mathbf s : \mathbf s}$, with $\mathbf s$ the deviatoric stress. It is compared with a yield or allowable stress.

**Reaction.** The force or moment a constraint exerts to hold its prescribed value. Reactions balance the applied loads.

**Receipt.** The record behind a number quoted in the docs: example id, result digest, CLI build, mesh and date, and the reference value it was compared with.

## Dynamics

**Natural frequency and mode shape.** The eigenvalues and eigenvectors of $(\mathbf K - \omega^2 \mathbf M)\,\boldsymbol\phi = \mathbf 0$.

**Rayleigh damping.** Damping proportional to mass and stiffness, $\mathbf C_d = \alpha \mathbf M + \beta \mathbf K$.

**Damping ratio.** The fraction $\zeta$ of critical damping applied to each mode.

## Execution

**CPU reference path.** The CPU execution provider, reported as `cpu_reference`. A study that asks for a GPU runs here when the GPU path can't run it, and reports a fallback diagnostic. `accelerators = required` makes such a study fail instead.

**Not yet in the current release.** A marker on a feature the docs describe but the current release does not include yet. Each marker names an id listed on the [release status](./validation/status.md) page.
